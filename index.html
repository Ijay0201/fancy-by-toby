Caring for Your Fancy

Protect your investment with these simple maintenance rituals.

cleaning_services

Gentle Cleaning

Use a soft-bristled brush and lukewarm water with a drop of mild soap to restore the original luster of your gold pieces.

inventory_2

Proper Storage

Store each piece individually in your Fancy by Toby pouch to prevent scratches and tangling of delicate chains.

block

Chemical Avoidance

Apply perfumes, lotions, and hairsprays before putting on your jewelry to protect the finish from harsh chemicals.
